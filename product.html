Built for the actual workflow

More than creating a bill.

Ice cream distribution involves stock arriving from the company, multiple shop orders, deliveries, partial payments, outstanding balances, product expiry and returns. The software keeps those activities connected instead of tracking them in separate notebooks or files.

🚚
Stock changes when delivery actually happens

An order can exist while delivery is still pending. Stock is deducted when the order is marked delivered rather than when the order is first created.

💰
Partial payments stay visible

Record payments against orders, keep the remaining outstanding amount visible and track it through the shop’s history.

🧊
Batch and expiry are part of stock receiving

Received stock can include invoice details, batch numbers and expiry dates, with low-stock and expiry alerts available in the system.

Real distribution situations

The software follows what happens after a shop calls.

These are the kinds of day-to-day situations the current system is built to handle.

01

A shop orders ice cream, but delivery is later.

Create the order and bill first. The order remains pending for delivery, and inventory is deducted only when the delivery is confirmed.

02

The shop pays only part of the bill.

Record the received amount while the balance remains outstanding. The distributor can review payment status, due dates and the shop’s running history.

03

New company stock arrives with batches and expiry dates.

Receive stock into inventory, capture invoice information, batch number and expiry date, then monitor low-stock and expiry alerts.

04

Damaged or expired items come back from a shop.

Returns are recorded separately with product, quantity, condition, reason and credit information rather than disappearing from the records.
